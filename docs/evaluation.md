# Evaluation on s2n-bignum

Figures recorded from a corpus check on s2n-bignum `4d1356a7` with HOL Light `cba9198`, every file indexed. The check script was removed in the lookup-only trim, and figures for the removed tools (Go to Definition, statements, tactics, hover, completion, selection) are dropped. What remains describes the live parser and index. Not re-measured here.

- Indexing: 798 files plus 109 HOL Light dependencies in about 1.8 s, 180 MB heap. One unresolved import, `x86/x86-insns.ml`, which is generated by `make` and absent from a fresh checkout.
- Definitions: every top-level binding at the start of a line is indexed, and every `prove`, `time prove`, `new_definition` and `define` binding has its kind and statement. The bindings the check reports as missing are all inside comments or `let _ = ...`.
- Definition sources: all 30,809 indexed definitions (s2n-bignum and the HOL Light files it loads) have a recorded source, and finding the references of every one took 22 s in total, at most 39 ms each. 429 items are longer than 20,000 characters: machine code byte lists (`*_mc`, up to 710,363 characters), long proofs, and HOL Light's `Divstep/hull_light.ml`, whose items are 2000-line `let ... in` chains. `max_chars` cuts them.
